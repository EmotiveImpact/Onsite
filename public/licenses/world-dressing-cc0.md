# Heron Reach world dressing

The scanned models in `models/heron-reach/heron-reach-world-props.glb` and
`heron-reach-world-plants.glb` originate from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com).
They are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Poly Haven's publisher licence statement: <https://polyhaven.com/license>.

The prop kit contains selected, adapted variants of `trashbag`,
`metal_trash_can`, `exterior_aircon_unit`, `cardboard_box_01`, `wooden_crate_02` and
`old_tyre`. Original publisher identities, creator credits and exact upstream
paths are recorded in `models/donors/provenance.json` and the existing
[donor notice](DONOR-ASSETS.md). These came through IRONLINE commit
`130ac2a5c856401ec36e7d35afcfefe40c5801f4`; its package licence is not the
basis for the individual asset permissions.

The plant kit contains:

- [Fern 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02), scanned by Rob Tuytel and modelled
  by Rico Cilliers. Four original variants, with the original opacity map.
- [Planter Pot Clay](https://polyhaven.com/a/planter_pot_clay), by Amal Kumar.

ONSITE prepared metre-scale individual Blender roots, closed the selected bin
lid, reduced texture sizes, combined fern colour and opacity, and added an
original fitted soil insert. No collision or navigation data comes from these
scans. Editable sources, source hashes, transformations and derived-file records
are in the repository's `assets/blender/heron-reach/world-props-manifest.json`,
`world-plants-manifest.json` and `assets/reference/world-dressing/source-manifest.json`.
