## 1. Wet ground and reflections

Branch: `codex/heron-reach-wet-ground` (base: `main`).

- Corrected lamp reflection receivers: read the top of the existing floor beneath each light.
  The old storey rounding put a 4.75 m street lamp's reflection at +3 m in the air, and a 2.72 m
  ceiling lamp's reflection on the floor above. Overlapping floors and B1 now select correctly.
- Asphalt, paving, red pavers and the court share a deterministic 11 m wetness mask. Shallow
  pools lower roughness and soften the normal response; exposed aggregate stays rougher.
- A broken, stretched lamp reflection replaces the smooth orange ellipse. It stays on the
  supporting floor and stretches towards the camera. The material reflects the existing dusk
  environment; these are **not scene-object planar/SSR reflections**. No second scene render.
- Two original procedural 512 px textures total 91,170 bytes. Their generator, hashes and
  provenance are recorded in `public/materials/heron/decals/wetness-manifest.json`.

Identical viewpoints are saved in `scripts/maps/art-review-views.json`; run
`OUT=test-results/art-review node scripts/maps/capture-art-review.mjs` against a production preview.
The report records requested and actual positions, renderer, errors and draw counters.

## 2. Warm lamp light and blue-hour atmosphere

Branch: `codex/heron-reach-atmosphere` (stacked on the wet-ground branch).

The sky now grades from slate blue to a cool grey horizon, with restrained warmth towards sunset.
The distant panorama is desaturated and participates in fog; geometry fades through blue-grey
distance haze. Softer cool fill reveals the brick and concrete without turning dusk into night.
Lamp selection accounts for contribution as well as distance, so small doorway lamps do not evict
all court columns from the limited light pool. Street pools reach further; ceiling strips have a
cooler fluorescent tint. Sign and lamp emission is reduced to retain lettering, and hardware bloom
has a higher threshold and lower strength (hardware appearance still requires verification).

Rain is shorter and fainter in the air, with a warm tint near the closest lamps. It still fades
under roofs. No new texture or model is introduced by this item.
