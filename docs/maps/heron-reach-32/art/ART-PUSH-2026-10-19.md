## 1. Wet ground and reflections

Branch: `codex/heron-reach-wet-ground` (base: `main`).

- Corrected lamp reflection receivers: read the top of the existing floor beneath each light.
  The old storey rounding put a 4.75 m street lamp's reflection at +3 m in the air, and a 2.72 m
  ceiling lamp's reflection on the floor above. Overlapping floors and B1 now select correctly.
- Asphalt, paving, red pavers and the court share a deterministic 11 m wetness mask. Shallow
  pools lower roughness and soften the normal response; exposed aggregate stays rougher.
- A broken, stretched lamp reflection replaces the smooth orange ellipse. It stays on the
  supporting floor and stretches towards the camera. The material reflects the existing dusk
  environment; these are **not scene-object planar/SSR reflections**. No second scene render.

## 2. Warm lamp light and blue-hour atmosphere

Branch: `codex/heron-reach-atmosphere` (stacked on the wet-ground branch).

Validation: typecheck, production build, all 41 tests, six matched views and a native-resolution
inspection view passed. Both full browser encounters passed: [Heron Reach](evidence/atmosphere/reach-browser.json)
(six bots, capture/reset, 84 health) and [Heron Court](evidence/atmosphere/court-browser.json)
(three bots, capture/reset, 76 health), with no page/request errors. Final software frame means
were 664.94 ms and 571.79 ms respectively; real-GPU performance remains unverified.

The sky now grades from slate blue to a cool grey horizon, with restrained warmth towards sunset.
The distant panorama is desaturated and participates in fog; geometry fades through blue-grey
distance haze. Softer cool fill reveals the brick and concrete without turning dusk into night.
Lamp selection accounts for contribution as well as distance, so small doorway lamps do not evict
all court columns from the limited light pool. Street pools reach further; ceiling strips have a
cooler fluorescent tint. Sign and lamp emission is reduced to retain lettering, and hardware bloom
has a higher threshold and lower strength (hardware appearance still requires verification).

Rain is shorter and fainter in the air, with a warm tint near the closest lamps. It still fades
under roofs. No new texture or model is introduced by this item.

## 7. Texture allocation, gallery netting and objective marker

Branch: `codex/heron-reach-textures-dressing` (stacked on skyline/trees).

The architecture library has 87 KTX2 alternatives with per-texture WebP fallback. Normal maps
use UASTC; colour and ORM use ETC1S. The compressed download increases from 20.04 MB to 34.71 MB
for this library; the benefit is smaller decoded GPU textures, to be checked in the browser and
reported separately from real-GPU performance. Original source art and WebPs remain intact. Production builds now exclude Blender
`*.raw.glb` intermediates even when those gitignored files exist in the local public directory;
the workspace copies are retained.
See [TEXTURES.md](TEXTURES.md) for sources, encoder and transcoder licences and exact byte counts.

A 104 KB / 1,008-triangle Blender kit places 12 pigeon-net panels above existing gallery parapets,
clear of doors and stair links. `gallery-detail.blend` and its manifest retain the editable geometry,
texture source hashes and world placements. The original generated net artwork is reused. The hardware postprocessing targets now request
supported 2× (or 4×) half-float MSAA so alpha-to-coverage has an actual multisampled target;
unsupported formats use fractional alpha instead. Canvas antialiasing alone does not cover the
postprocessing target. This hardware path and its allocation cost remain unverified.

The HUD now projects an amber diamond over The Cage, with distance and a ground-floor qualifier
when seen from another storey. It changes to HOLD THE COURT once the bots are cleared. Projection
is presentation-only; the existing encounter still owns capture eligibility and round completion.

All 43 tests, typecheck and the production build passed. Eight matched runtime views passed
without page, console or request errors. The marker is visible with distance at The Cage, and
identifies GROUND FLOOR when viewed from the gallery. Both full input-driven browser encounters passed with no page/request errors:
[Heron Reach](evidence/textures/reach-browser.json), nine checks, six bots, capture/reset, 60 health;
[Heron Court](evidence/textures/court-browser.json), ten checks, three bots, capture/reset, 96 health.
Final rolling software frame means were **680.89 ms** (360 samples, about 1.47 FPS) and
**563.57 ms** (156 samples, about 1.77 FPS). These are slow SwiftShader results, not hardware
measurements or controlled before/after benchmarks. Real-GPU performance remains unverified.
