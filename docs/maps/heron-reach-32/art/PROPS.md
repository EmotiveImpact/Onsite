# Heron Reach props kit

Street furniture, the Cage (the estate's fenced five-a-side court), parked vehicles, shop,
launderette and youth club fixtures, and litter for the Heron Reach estate. Everything in the
kit is original work, generated by the scripts listed below from Python code (bmesh geometry,
numpy and Pillow textures). Nothing was downloaded, traced, scanned or adapted from
third-party models or textures.

Rebuild, in order, from the repository root:

```
python3 scripts/maps/art/make_heron_prop_textures.py          # about 25 s
python3 scripts/maps/art/make_heron_props.py                  # about 3 s
python3 scripts/maps/art/export_heron_props_test.py OUT_DIR    # optional GLB check
python3 scripts/maps/art/render_heron_props.py final OUT_DIR [street,cage,shop,interiors,vehicles]
python3 scripts/maps/art/render_heron_props.py inspect OUT.png NAME[,NAME] [--angle DEG]
```

Every generator uses fixed seeds, so a rebuild gives the same textures, meshes and manifest.
`make_heron_props.py --only NAME[,NAME] --out PATH` builds a subset into a scratch file and
leaves the manifest alone. The builder starts from an empty file, so do not hand-edit
`props-kit.blend`: edit the scripts and rebuild.

## Conventions used

- Metres, Blender Z up, front faces -Y, transforms applied, no negative scale.
- Ground assets: origin at the centre of the footprint on the ground (Z = 0).
- Wall assets (`"mount": "wall"` in the manifest): origin on the wall face (Y = 0), at the
  fixing centre given in the asset's notes, projecting toward -Y. Place them on a wall with
  the wall's outward normal as the asset's -Y. `DOWNPIPE_3M` has its origin at ground level
  on the wall face.
- Modular pieces tile along +X at 1 m (`KERB_1M`, `PLANTER_EDGE`, `RAILING_1M`) or 2.5 m
  (`CAGE_PANEL_2_5M` between `CAGE_POST` pieces). `RAILING_1M` carries its post on the -X end;
  close a run with `RAILING_POST`. `KERB_1M` has its road face at -Y and its top at pavement
  level, 0.125 m.
- `LAMP_COLUMN`, `WALL_LAMP`, `SHOP_FRIDGE` and `SHOP_SIGN_CORNER` carry a `lightAnchor`
  in the manifest (asset space, metres) for placing a runtime light.
- Edges are bevelled where they catch light and marked sharp by angle (about 40 degrees) and at
  every material boundary, so glTF export splits normals cleanly.

## Materials and textures

Shared materials used by the kit (`hr_steel_black`, `hr_steel_galv`, `hr_steel_aged`,
`hr_concrete_cast`, `hr_concrete_rough`, `hr_timber`, `hr_plastic_black`, `hr_plastic_green`,
`hr_paint_yellow`, `hr_lamp_emit`, `hr_glass`, `hr_upvc_white`) are plain Principled
placeholders with the correct names. Tyres and moulded rubber use the kit's own
`hr_prop_tyre`, because the shared `hr_rubber` is a rubber floor tile. Swap in the texture
kit's versions from `assets/blender/heron-reach/materials.blend` by name; the preview script
does exactly this with `user_remap`. Faces using them carry metre UVs (1 UV unit = 1 m,
box projected per face), so the tiling textures land at real scale.

`hr_prop_*` materials are owned by this kit. Those with images use 0 to 1 UVs into these
textures in `public/materials/heron/props/`:

Alpha-clipped materials wire the texture's alpha through a Round node into the BSDF Alpha,
so they export as glTF `MASK` (no `alphaCutoff` is written, which means the glTF default of 0.5). Fridge glass and the notice board
cover use a constant alpha and export as `BLEND`. Lenses, the shop sign letters, fridge
strips and the fridge header are emissive and export with `KHR_materials_emissive_strength`.

The builder turns on back-face culling for every material whose faces are closed shells in
all assets that use it; materials on open sheets, decal patches, alpha cards and glass stay
double-sided. The manifest lists the double-sided ones under `doubleSidedMaterials`. The one
deliberately inward-facing shell is the dark pit box under the `DRAIN_GULLY` grating.

## Collision

Art never changes the level's collision. The manifest gives, for each asset,
`needsCollision` and a simple `collider` in asset space (`box` with centre and size, or
`cylinder` with centre, radius and height, or a list of boxes) for the level generator.

- Needs collision: `LAMP_COLUMN`, `BOLLARD`, `LITTER_BIN`, both wheelie bins,
  `EURO_BIN_1100`, `BENCH`, `CYCLE_HOOP`, `UTILITY_CABINET`, `PLANTER_EDGE`, `RAILING_1M`,
  `RAILING_POST`, `SIGN_POST`, `NOTICE_BOARD`, `ROOF_VENT`, `ROOF_TANK`, `CAGE_POST`,
  `CAGE_PANEL_2_5M`, `CAGE_GATE_4M` (four boxes: both posts and both open leaves; the header is overhead),
  `GOAL_FIVE_A_SIDE`, `CAR_HATCHBACK` and `CAR_HATCHBACK_FAR` (two boxes: body and cabin),
  `VAN_DELIVERY` and `VAN_DELIVERY_FAR`, every shop and interior fixture, and `SHOPPING_TROLLEY`.
- Render only: wall-mounted items, `KERB_1M` (the level owns the kerb step), `DRAIN_GULLY`,
  `MANHOLE`, `BIKE` (thin; a box is given if wanted), `BALL`, `TRAFFIC_CONE` and the
  litter. `BIN_BAG_C` and `MATTRESS_DUMPED` can take a low box if they should block.

## Invented names and lettering

All brands, labels and signs are invented for Heron Reach: THE CORNER (FOOD & WINE),
HERON and REACH own-label packs, KORA COLA, KRUNCHO, VOLTEDGE, the Heron Reach Estate
notices, the estate sign and HERON HOUSE FLATS 1 TO 48. No real brands, logos or trademarks appear. Vehicles
are generic shapes with no maker badges; the number plate `HR18 0NS` uses a zero where a
real registration needs a letter, so it cannot match a real plate. Lettering was rasterised
from the system Inter and DejaVu fonts (both under open licences) and the 3D sign letters
were built from Inter Display; no font files are shipped.

## Known limits

### Cage detail kit, 19 October 2026

`scripts/maps/art/build_heron_cage_detail.py` builds the separate editable
`assets/blender/heron-reach/cage-detail.blend` and the runtime `heron-reach-cage.glb`,
using the original ONSITE `heron_props_cage.py` generators. It preserves the older kit files.
The session has Blender 4.3.2, which cannot open the newer source `.blend` header, so this kit is
generated from the existing source code rather than converting or overwriting the original.

The kit has 5,636 triangles: existing posts, panels, gates and goals, a ball, 6.5 m paired
floodlights and render-only court paint. Green frames retain their original footprints and gates.
The four floodlights replace lamp render meshes at the existing court lamp positions; the
original dressing plan and all collision remain unchanged. Pitch paint follows the as-built
offset goals, which leave the central gates open. It is a five-a-side-inspired estate court,
not a regulation pitch. Nothing creates a new physical obstacle.

`make_heron_prop_textures.py --cage-only` draws the finer diagonal chain-link and goal net maps
from the project's periodic wire/noise functions. These are original procedural pixels, with
hashes in `cage-detail-manifest.json`. The runtime uses mipmaps and anisotropy; hardware uses
alpha-to-coverage, while the software fallback retains fractional coverage with blending.

Rebuild and compress without opening a newer `.blend`:

```sh
python3 scripts/maps/art/make_heron_prop_textures.py --cage-only
blender -b --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/maps/art/build_heron_cage_detail.py
npx gltfpack -i public/models/heron-reach/heron-reach-cage.raw.glb -o public/models/heron-reach/heron-reach-cage.glb -cc -kn -km -kv -vtf
```

### Shop detail kit, 19 October 2026

`assets/blender/heron-reach/shop-detail.blend` is an original procedural assembly made with
Blender 4.3.2 by `build_heron_shop_detail.py`. It adds single-sided image backdrops at the rear
walls of The Corner, the Launderette and the Washhouse, plus high-level strip fittings. The
2.1 m backdrop gaps preserve rear exits. No collision, navigation or existing prop changes.
Image provenance is in [TEXTURES.md](TEXTURES.md) and `public/materials/heron/shops/manifest.json`.

```sh
python3 scripts/maps/art/make_heron_shop_textures.py
blender -b --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/maps/art/build_heron_shop_detail.py
npx gltfpack -i public/models/heron-reach/heron-reach-shops.raw.glb -o public/models/heron-reach/heron-reach-shops.glb -cc -kn -km -kv -vtf
```

- The shared materials in `props-kit.blend` are placeholders until swapped.
- The weld mesh and wire textures are fine detail; at long range they need mipmapping with
  alpha-to-coverage, or a fade to a solid lower-opacity material, to avoid shimmer.
- The vehicles are generic lofted bodies tuned to read as a hatchback and a panel van; they
  have no opening doors and a simple interior.


## Gallery pigeon-net detail kit

Original ONSITE supports/cables around the existing upper gallery parapets. Twelve panels, 1,008
triangles, 104 KB compressed GLB, with no collision or navigation geometry. Artwork is the
project's existing generated pigeon-net PNG, retained at its original path. The manifest records
source/derived texture hashes, metre-scale world placements and the Blender version.

```sh
blender -b --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/maps/art/build_heron_gallery_detail.py
npx gltfpack -i public/models/heron-reach/heron-reach-gallery.raw.glb -o public/models/heron-reach/heron-reach-gallery.glb -cc -kn -km -kv -vtf
```

The additive editable source is `assets/blender/heron-reach/gallery-detail.blend`. The runtime
loads it separately, using fractional alpha coverage on software and alpha-to-coverage on hardware.
No doors, gallery junctions or stairs are closed by this cosmetic layer.
