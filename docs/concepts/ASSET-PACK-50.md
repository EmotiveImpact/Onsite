# ONSITE — 50 asset references

Fifty individual modelling reference images, continuing the black clothing and trainers, weathered London estate materials, and white/amber ONSITE presentation.

These are concept references, not finished meshes, textures, rigs or playable assets. Views and printed dimensions may need reconciliation during modelling. See the [modelling handoff](MODELLING-NOTES.md) and [earlier character sheets](CHARACTER-ASSET-GALLERY.md).
